How to measure

🧵

Chest / Bust

Wrap the tape around the fullest part, under your arms. Keep it level, not tight.

🎯

Waist

Measure around your natural waist — the narrowest part of your torso.

📐

Hip / Length

Hips: fullest part around. Length: shoulder to where you want the hem to fall.
